feat(meta,cmd): cap the number of snapshots a volume holds

Snapshots cost inodes and are exempt from quotas, so a MaxSnapshots
format setting, 0 for unlimited, set with juicefs format or config
--max-snapshots, bounds how much metadata they add. Both refuse a
negative value. The count is the nlink of the hidden root.
CreateSnapshot checks it before copying, and each engine checks it
again in the attach transaction, so two creates racing at the limit
cannot both pass.
