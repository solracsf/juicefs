feat(meta): keep snapshots through gc, compaction, dump and load

- The Redis leaked-inode sweep leaves snapshot roots alone, which have no
  directory entry by design, and the inodes of a snapshot being built,
  which carry the old ctime of what they copy and may not be named yet.
- Compaction skips FlagSnapshot inodes: reading a snapshot schedules it
  like any file, and merging its slices would rewrite what it captured.
  The check runs after the cheap structural tests, so it costs a read
  only when a compaction would otherwise go ahead.
- The JSON dump writes the hidden root as a third "Snapshots" section,
  from the fast and normal paths of every engine, and the loader rebuilds
  nextSnapshot from the snapshot roots. A client that predates this
  ignores the section. The protobuf backup saves nextSnapshot, which it
  lacked, so a restore cannot hand out the inode of an existing
  snapshot, and keeps snapshot roots out of nextTrash.
- A dump whose --subdir points into /.snapshots is refused before
  anything is written: loaded, its frozen entries could never be changed
  or emptied. Clone the snapshot into a live directory and dump that.
