feat(vfs): serve and list /.snapshots

Snapshot roots live above minInternalNode, so IsSpecialNode excludes
their range: otherwise the VFS treats them as internal files, and
getxattr, listxattr, flock, locks and ioctl fail on them.

.snapshots appears in the root listing like .trash, once the first
snapshot has created it and only on a mount of the whole volume, where
the name resolves. HideInternal hides it and PrefixInternal renames it
to .jfs.snapshots, as for .trash.

A path into a hidden root is looked up entry by entry even with fast
resolve on: the fast resolve of the Redis engine, used by the Java SDK,
walks directory entries in a Lua script, and the hidden roots have none.
