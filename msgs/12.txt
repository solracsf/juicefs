feat(meta,cmd): exempt snapshots from capacity and quotas

Charging a snapshot against the volume capacity and the directory,
user and group quotas would stop a volume more than half full from
taking a whole-volume snapshot and double df after one, and every
recount walks from the root, never reaches .snapshots, and would drop
the charge again. So snapshot clone mode skips the stat, quota and
user/group updates, and the JSON and protobuf loaders leave FlagSnapshot
inodes out of the usage they rebuild. A directory quota on the snapshot
root, a snapshot or anything inside one is refused, as one on the trash
is.

snapshot list reports the usage instead: inodes and size per snapshot
and a total. Each snapshot directory resyncs its dir stat from what was
copied, since the row copied from the source can lag behind its
buffered updates and would be frozen wrong.
